How to play

Your run starts in free fall. Catch a rooftop before you reach the street. Ground contact ends the run.

← → / ↑ ↓Look, move or choose a ledgeHold left/right to pan the view on rooftops or in the air. On rooftops, up/down run forward/backward in the viewing direction. In the air: Up selects the best swing ahead of the camera; down selects the best swing behind. Recommendations account for momentum, spring physics and predicted clearance. If none of the nearby points is in that direction, your selection stays unchanged. The camera pulls back to highlight your selection; the diamond marks the selected ledge.
SPACESwing and soarHold to connect. A new airborne connection resets the view to your direction of travel. Release to fly. Press up/down while holding to transfer to another ledge.
ESCTake a breatherPause. Then Space continues or Esc returns to the title screen.

Mobile: drag the joystick to turn. Tap Select to highlight the next ledge ahead, then hold Swing to attach and release to fly.
