How to play

Pony Express: start on a rooftop, then follow the searchlight to collect the parcel on a rooftop, then land inside the marked circle on the destination building. Free roam starts in free fall. Ground contact ends the run.

← → / A DSteerTurn your travel direction. On rooftops, Up/Down or W/S move forward/back. Swing anchors follow your travel intent automatically.
SPACESwingHold to catch a building. Release to preserve your current trajectory. No manual ledge selection is needed.
SHIFTJump / Zip / LaunchJump from a roof or swing. In flight, zip to the centered diamond if shown, otherwise air zip forward. Press again near perch arrival to launch. Once perched, run around the rooftop with the movement keys; its edges keep you safe. Press Shift to launch off.
LOOKFollowing cameraRight-drag with a mouse, or drag the open scene on touch. Q recenters. The camera follows travel direction; steering recenters your view. Looking does not change targeting.
C / ESCDive / pauseC dives in free flight. Esc pauses; Space continues.

Mobile: joystick left/right steers; up/down moves on rooftops. The camera follows. Hold Swing and use the second button for Jump, Zip, or Launch. Drag the scene to look optionally.
